# EinthusanTV brand asset library

This folder is the canonical home for approved EinthusanTV logo and brand artwork. Keep future logo concepts, approved masters, app icons, splash artwork and Android TV banners here so they are easy to find and version.

## Current approved master design

The approved master is the artwork supplied by the project owner in this conversation on 27 November 2026:
- Dark near-black cinematic background with subtle purple/magenta glow and light sweep.
- Large flowing gradient play-symbol: purple through magenta/red to orange, with a white play triangle.
- Bold white `Einthusan` wordmark and orange-to-pink gradient `TV`.
- Preserve the exact supplied artwork; do not substitute the older rounded-square play glyph or recreate the wordmark with app text.

**Original image file:** pending binary upload as `einthusantv-master-logo.jpg`. The GitHub file actions available here can create and edit text files but cannot directly transfer the chat's binary image attachment, so the original artwork has not yet been copied into the repository.

## Asset naming convention

- `einthusantv-master-logo.jpg` — approved full master artwork
- `einthusantv-logo-transparent.png` — transparent logo variant, if supplied
- `einthusantv-app-icon.png` — square launcher artwork
- `einthusantv-tv-banner.png` — Android TV / Fire TV banner
- `einthusantv-splash.png` — opening splash artwork
- `concepts/YYYY-MM-DD-name` — unapproved explorations

## Rules

1. Preserve original source artwork; do not overwrite it with resized/cropped variants.
2. Keep generated platform variants alongside the master and document dimensions/cropping.
3. Clearly label concepts as **DRAFT** and the selected asset as **APPROVED**.
4. Use the approved master consistently across splash, navigation, sign-in, settings, launcher icon and TV banner.
5. Check launcher safe zones and avoid adding black side bars around the icon artwork.
